Biography

Anisha Shrivastav is an officer of the Indian Administrative Service, of the 2021 batch and the Madhya Pradesh cadre. Her service has spanned revenue and district administration, the administration of a Union Territory, and the industrial development of one of central India’s principal regions.

She currently serves as Executive Director of the M.P. Industrial Development Corporation at Gwalior, the Government of Madhya Pradesh’s industrial development agency for the Gwalior and Chambal divisions. The office develops and administers the region’s industrial estates, allots industrial land, and takes manufacturers from first enquiry through clearance to commissioning: land, approvals, power, water, and the steady resolution of whatever stands between an investment decision and an operating plant.

She read commerce at Shri Ram College of Commerce, Delhi, and entered the Service in 2021 through the Civil Services Examination, in which she secured All India Rank 66.
